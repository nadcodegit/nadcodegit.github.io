Where agents help most

Common areas small businesses hand off to us — if it’s repetitive and rule-based, there’s probably an agent for it.

Customer chat & DMs
agent replying

Replying to the same questions on Instagram, WhatsApp or your website chat, over and over. The agent answers instantly and only hands you the ones that actually need you.

Scheduling & calendar
booking confirmed

Back-and-forth messages just to lock in a time. The agent checks your calendar, offers real slots, and books it — no more chasing replies.

Invoicing & billing
invoice sent

Building the same invoice by hand every month, then chasing people to pay it. The agent generates it, sends it, and follows up for you.

Email inbox triage
reply drafted

An inbox full of things that all need a reply eventually. The agent sorts what’s urgent, drafts responses, and only flags what truly needs your judgment.

Data entry & reports
report updated

Copying the same numbers from one spreadsheet or system into another, every single week. The agent pulls the data and keeps your report current without you touching it.

Order & inventory tracking
stock updated

Checking stock by hand and updating it after every sale. The agent tracks inventory in real time and tells you when it’s time to reorder.

Lead follow-ups
follow-up sent

A new lead comes in and then nothing, because you got busy. The agent follows up right away and keeps following up until they respond.

Social media replies
comment answered

Comments and DMs piling up on posts you don’t have time to check. The agent replies to common questions so nobody gets ignored.
