Scheduling & Invoicing Agent

The Problem

Freelancers and small teams lose real time every month rebuilding the same invoice from scratch — reading through job emails, checking what got done, and putting it all together by hand.

Before

Manually reading job emails, cross-checking the calendar, and building the invoice in a spreadsheet or document — every single month.

The Solution

A CrewAI-based agent reads the job emails, updates the calendar, and drafts the invoice automatically — every time, on its own.

Business Value

Fewer manual steps each month, a more consistent invoice every time, and one less recurring task on your plate.

Instagram Sales Assistant

A flooded DM inbox, and a shop to run.

The Problem

Customers ask the same questions on Instagram DMs — about products, prices, availability, options and orders — day and night, while you’re busy running the shop itself.

Before

Answering DMs late into the night, one at a time, and losing track of who asked what and whether their order ever got handled.

The Solution

A LangGraph agent reads every DM as it comes in, checks the relevant information, and takes care of it — day or night.

Customer sends a message ↓ AI understands the request ↓ Checks relevant information ↓ Takes the appropriate action ↓ Updates the conversation & order state ↓ Responds to the customer ↓ Hands off to a human when needed
Business Value

Faster replies at any hour, fewer missed messages, and a consistent, organized conversation for every customer.

Data Analysis Assistant

The Problem

Getting a straight answer out of a spreadsheet usually means digging through rows and formulas yourself, or waiting on whoever built the sheet.

Before

The Solution

Business Value

Faster answers straight from your own data, less waiting on someone else, and a tool your whole team can use without spreadsheet skills.
